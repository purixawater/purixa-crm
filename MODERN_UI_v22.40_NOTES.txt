PURIXA CRM v22.40 — GLASSMORPHISM UI

This revision is a visual/UI layer applied to the v22.38 CRM.
- Existing JavaScript/Firebase/business logic was not intentionally changed.
- Existing CRM menus, dashboard sections, data IDs and invoice markup are retained.
- Glassmorphism background, sidebar, top bar, cards, panels, forms and modals added.
- Dashboard metric cards are more compact: 6 columns on wide desktop, 4 on medium, 2 on mobile.
- Mobile navigation remains available.

Backup/base: Purixa CRM v22.38 supplied by the user.
