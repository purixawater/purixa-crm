Purixa CRM v22.68 — Service + AMC final logic

1. Completed AMC visits and completed Customer Services share one customer next-service schedule: latest completed visit date + 3 months.
2. Completed AMC visits no longer use a conflicting manual next-service date.
3. AMC planned next-service date is synced to the Customer record before the first completed AMC visit.
4. Stale customer AMC end dates are cleared automatically when no matching AMC record exists.
5. AMC edit/renew/save paths recalculate schedule consistently.
6. PWA/service-worker version/cache bumped to v22.68 to prevent stale v22.66 cache loading.
