Purixa CRM v22.34

New features:
- Expenses module with date, category, description, amount, payment mode and notes.
- Monthly expense filter and search.
- Expense edit/delete.
- Expense summary: Revenue, Expenses, Estimated Profit, Entry count.
- Reports now include Total Revenue, Expenses and Estimated Profit.
- Firebase normalized sync/load includes expenses.
- Existing v22.33 modules and data structure preserved.

Important:
Estimated Profit = Sales Total + Completed Service Revenue + AMC Revenue - recorded Expenses.
Inventory cost is not automatically included as an expense unless recorded in Expenses.
Backup v22.33 remains unchanged.
